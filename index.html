About us

An engineering-led consultancy

Rey Engineering Solutions Ltd is a private limited company providing information technology consultancy. The company was incorporated in July 2014 and has its registered office in Harrow, England.

Our approach is straightforward: understand the problem properly, recommend what genuinely fits, and stay practical about how it gets built and run.
